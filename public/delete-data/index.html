The game: Changa Chett: Kite Fight by Peter Karki (com.pinological.changachett).

If you submitted a score, our server holds your gamer name, a random player id, and your scores and run details (wave, kills, kite, map, game version, date). Deleting removes all of it, straight away and for good. Progress saved on your phone is not touched; uninstalling the game removes that.

Three ways to delete

  1. In the game: Profile → Delete my online data.
  2. Here: use the form below.
  3. By email: write to peter.karki0422@gmail.com with your player id and gamer name. We reply within 30 days.

The name is only there so you can check you typed the right id. Deletion uses the player id. Lost your id? Email us your gamer name and tag (like Sita#4F2A) and we will remove it by hand.
