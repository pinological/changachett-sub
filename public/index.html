Comms सम्पर्क

How do I install the APK?

Download it from itch.io on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser or file manager, then tap Install.

Is it free? Ads?

Free and offline. Ads show on the menu and game-over screens only, never during play.

I have the itch.io version. Can I switch to Google Play?

Yes. The itch.io and Play versions are signed differently, so uninstall the itch.io one first, then install from Google Play.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 7.0 or newer, 64-bit. Most phones from the last few years are fine.

What data do you collect?

Progress stays on your phone. If you submit a score to the leaderboard, we store your gamer name, a random player id and your run details; you can delete them any time. Ads come from Google AdMob. Read the privacy policy.
