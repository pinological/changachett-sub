# Privacy Policy: Changa Chett: Kite Fight

**Policy version:** 3. **Effective:** 20 October 2026. **Developer:** Peter Karki, Nepal. **Contact:** peter.karki0422@gmail.com. **Package:** com.pinological.changachett.
Published at https://changachett.peterkarki.com.np/privacy (that page is the source of truth; keep this file in step).

1. **What stays on your phone.** No account, no login. Progress and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling deletes them.
2. **Online leaderboard (only if you tap Submit).** We store your chosen gamer name, a random player id (not linked to your identity), and scores and run details (wave, kills, kite, map, version, date). Name, 4-character tag, best score, wave and date are public on the leaderboard. Rate limiting uses a scrambled network address kept for one hour. Kept until you ask for deletion (in-game button, /delete-data, or email). Stored in a MongoDB Atlas database (data hosted by MongoDB, Inc.; the website and API run on Vercel).
3. **Advertising.** Google AdMob, with Unity Ads through AdMob mediation; menu and game-over screens plus optional rewarded video. They may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device and usage data, and may show personalised ads. https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. EEA/UK/Switzerland users get a consent prompt (Google UMP), changeable in settings.
4. **What we do not do.** No sale of data, no account, no location collection, no sharing beyond the ad, database and hosting providers named above.
5. **Children.** For ages 13 and over; not directed at children under 13.
6. **This website.** No cookies, trackers or analytics; one session-storage flag to skip the intro.
7. **Beta testers.** Tester-form details are used only for the Google Play test and deleted after it, or sooner on request.
8. **Your rights.** Access, correction and deletion on request: peter.karki0422@gmail.com (reply within 30 days).
9. **Changes.** Posted on this page, with a new version and date, before the change goes live.
