# Privacy Policy: Changa Chett: Kite Fight

**Policy version:** 5. **Effective:** 8 November 2026 (game version 1.0.5). **Developer:** Peter Karki, Nepal. **Contact:** peter.karki0422@gmail.com. **Package:** com.pinological.changachett.
Published at https://changachett.peterkarki.com.np/privacy (that page is the source of truth; keep this file in step).

1. **Guest by default, what stays on your phone.** You can play as a Guest: no account, no login. Progress and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling deletes them.
2. **Optional Google account (Android).** If you choose "Continue with Google" or "Link Google", Google shows its own account picker. The game sends the Google sign-in token to our server, which checks it with Google and keeps only a one-way hash of your Google account ID, linked to your player id, so your leaderboard scores and name come back if you change phone. We do not store your email address, Google account ID, Google name or the token. Your Google display name is used only on your phone to suggest a gamer name. The link is removed when you delete your data. You can always stay a Guest.
3. **Online leaderboard (only if you tap Submit).** We store your chosen gamer name, a random player id (not linked to your identity), and scores and run details (wave, kills, kite, map, version, date). Name, 4-character tag, best score, wave and date are public on the leaderboard. Rate limiting uses a scrambled network address kept for one hour. Kept until you ask for deletion (in-game button, /delete-data, or email). Stored in a MongoDB Atlas database (data hosted by MongoDB, Inc.; the website and API run on Vercel).
4. **Advertising.** Google AdMob only; banner on menu and game-over screens plus optional rewarded video. Google may collect the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information and ad interactions, and may show personalised ads. https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. EEA/UK/Switzerland users get a consent prompt (Google UMP), changeable in settings.
5. **What we do not do.** No sale of data, no required account, no location collection, no sharing beyond Google (ads) and the database and hosting providers named above.
6. **Children.** For ages 13 and over; not directed at children under 13.
7. **This website.** No cookies, trackers or analytics; one session-storage flag to skip the intro.
8. **Your rights.** Access, correction and deletion on request: peter.karki0422@gmail.com (reply within 30 days).
9. **Changes.** Posted on this page, with a new version and date, before the change goes live.
